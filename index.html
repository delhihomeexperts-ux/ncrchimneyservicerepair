2. Chimney Noise Problem - Complete Guide 1000 Words

Chimney noise is a common complaint in Noida Ghaziabad apartments where chimney is used 2-3 hours daily. Noise sources include impeller imbalance due to uneven grease deposit, dry motor bearings, loose mounting screws, duct pipe vibration touching false ceiling, foreign object inside impeller like small screw or plastic, capacitor weak causing humming, motor winding short causing buzzing, fan blade hitting housing due to bent shaft, auto-clean heating element loose, filter not locked properly, and wall bracket loose. Our noise diagnosis starts with power off visual check, manual impeller rotation test, bearing play check, mounting level check using spirit level, duct touch test while running, voltage check, capacitor microfarad test, and sound frequency analysis to identify if noise is mechanical or electrical. Solution includes complete deep cleaning to remove imbalance, bearing greasing with high temperature grease, bearing replacement with SKF original, impeller replacement if bent, shaft straightening, duct damping with foam tape, rubber grommet installation between bracket and wall, capacitor replacement with correct rating, motor rewinding if winding short, filter lock replacement, and final decibel test at all speeds. Noise should be less than 58 decibels at speed 3 for normal chimney. If noise is more than 70 decibels, immediate service required otherwise motor will seize and cost will increase from 800 to 3500. Common mistakes by users include running chimney without filter which causes impeller grease deposition faster, using chimney at speed 1 only which causes oil accumulation, not cleaning filter for 6 months, putting heavy items on chimney top, and installing chimney with flexible duct instead of rigid duct which vibrates more. Our technicians in Indirapuram, Vaishali, Vasundhara, Crossing Republik carry noise meter, vibration meter, spare bearings, spare impellers for all major brands, and complete tool kit. Service time for noise issue is 30 to 60 minutes. 90 days warranty on noise repair. Preventive tips include cleaning filter every 10 days with hot water, running chimney for 2 minutes after cooking to remove residual smoke, checking duct outside cowl every 6 months, not using chimney as storage shelf, and booking deep cleaning every 4 months. Noise free chimney means peaceful kitchen conversation, no headache, and better cooking experience.
