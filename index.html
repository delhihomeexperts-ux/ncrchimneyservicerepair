Chimney Deep Cleaning - Complete Guide

Chimney deep cleaning is most critical for Noida Ghaziabad kitchens where daily tadka, frying, masala creates thick black sticky layer on filters, impeller, oil tray, smoke chamber, motor housing, duct joints. This reduces suction up to 70%, causes oil dripping, burning smell, motor failure. Our protocol includes complete disassembly, industrial chemical degreasing, hot soak 20 min, impeller removal, foam spray, scrubbing, hot water rinse, compressed air drying, motor dust removal, oil tray wash, duct cleaning, reassembly, suction test on all speeds, auto-clean test, body polish, report. Benefits: factory suction restored, oil dripping stops, motor life +2 years, electricity reduces, hygiene improves, fire risk zero. We serve Faber, Hindware, Kaff, Glen, Elica, Bosch, IFB, Kuchina, Sunflame, Bajaj, Prestige. 45-90 min, 45 days warranty.

Chimney Noise Problem - Complete Guide

Noise due to impeller imbalance from grease, dry bearings, loose screws, duct vibration touching ceiling, foreign object, weak capacitor, winding short, bent shaft, loose heating element, filter not locked. Diagnosis: visual check, manual rotation, bearing play, spirit level, duct touch, voltage, capacitor test. Fix: deep cleaning, greasing with SKF grease, bearing replacement, impeller replacement, foam damping, rubber grommet, capacitor replacement, motor rewinding. Noise should be under 58 dB at speed 3. Tips: clean filter every 10 days hot water, run 2 min after cooking, check outside cowl 6 months, don't use as shelf, book deep cleaning every 4 months.
