Chimney Deep Cleaning - Complete Guide

Chimney deep cleaning is the most critical maintenance for any Indian kitchen in Noida and Ghaziabad where daily cooking involves high oil, tadka, frying, and masala. Over time, grease particles mix with dust and form a thick, black, sticky layer on baffle filters, mesh filters, carbon filters, impeller blades, oil collector tray, smoke chamber, motor housing, and duct pipe joints. This layer reduces suction power by up to 70 percent, causes oil dripping from the chimney body, increases motor load, creates burning smell, and leads to motor bearing failure. Our professional deep cleaning protocol includes complete partial disassembly, industrial grade non-caustic chemical degreasing, soaking filters in hot chemical solution, impeller removal, foam spray inside smoke chamber, scrubbing, hot water high pressure rinse, compressed air drying, motor housing dust removal, oil tray washing, duct joint cleaning, gasket check, reassembly as per manufacturer torque, suction testing on all speeds, auto-clean cycle test, touch panel protection, exterior polishing, glass cleaning, and final performance report. We serve all brands including Faber, Hindware, Kaff, Glen, Elica, Bosch, IFB, Kuchina, Sunflame, Bajaj, Prestige.

Chimney Noise Problem - Complete Guide

Chimney noise is a common complaint in Noida Ghaziabad apartments where chimney is used 2-3 hours daily. Noise sources include impeller imbalance due to uneven grease deposit, dry motor bearings, loose mounting screws, duct pipe vibration touching false ceiling, foreign object inside impeller, capacitor weak causing humming, motor winding short causing buzzing, fan blade hitting housing, auto-clean heating element loose, filter not locked properly, and wall bracket loose. Our noise diagnosis starts with power off visual check, manual impeller rotation test, bearing play check, mounting level check, duct touch test while running, voltage check, capacitor test. Solution includes deep cleaning, bearing greasing, bearing replacement with SKF original, impeller replacement, duct damping with foam tape, rubber grommet installation, capacitor replacement, motor rewinding if needed.
