Chimney Deep Cleaning - Complete Guide

Chimney deep cleaning is the most critical maintenance for any Indian kitchen in Noida and Ghaziabad where daily cooking involves high oil, tadka, frying, and masala. Over time, grease particles mix with dust and form a thick, black, sticky layer on baffle filters, mesh filters, carbon filters, impeller blades, oil collector tray, smoke chamber, motor housing, and duct pipe joints. This layer reduces suction power by up to 70 percent, causes oil dripping from the chimney body, increases motor load, creates burning smell, and leads to motor bearing failure. Our professional deep cleaning protocol includes complete partial disassembly of all removable parts, industrial grade non-caustic chemical degreasing, soaking filters in hot chemical solution for 20 minutes, impeller removal using special puller, foam spray inside smoke chamber, scrubbing with nylon brush, hot water high pressure rinse, compressed air drying to prevent water short circuit, motor housing dust removal, oil tray washing, duct joint cleaning, gasket condition check, reassembly as per manufacturer torque, suction testing on all three speeds using anemometer, auto-clean cycle test for filterless models, touch panel protection during wash, exterior body polishing, glass cleaning, and final performance report with before-after photos. Benefits include factory level suction restored, no more smoke in kitchen, oil dripping stops, motor life increases by 2 years, electricity consumption reduces, kitchen hygiene improves, and fire risk reduces. We use eco-friendly, food-grade degreaser safe for kitchen environment. Same day service available if booking before 2 PM. Chimney Noise Problem - Complete Guide

Chimney noise is a common complaint in Noida Ghaziabad apartments where chimney is used 2-3 hours daily. Noise sources include impeller imbalance due to uneven grease deposit, dry motor bearings, loose mounting screws, duct pipe vibration touching false ceiling, foreign object inside impeller, capacitor weak causing humming, motor winding short causing buzzing, fan blade hitting housing due to bent shaft, auto-clean heating element loose, filter not locked properly, and wall bracket loose. Our noise diagnosis starts with power off visual check, manual impeller rotation test, bearing play check, mounting level check using spirit level, duct touch test while running, voltage check, capacitor test, and sound frequency analysis. Solution includes complete deep cleaning to remove imbalance, bearing greasing with high temperature grease, bearing replacement with SKF original, impeller replacement if bent, duct damping with foam tape, rubber grommet installation, capacitor replacement, motor rewinding if needed, filter lock replacement, and final decibel test. Noise should be less than 58 decibels at speed 3. Preventive tips include cleaning filter every 10 days with hot water, running chimney for 2 minutes after cooking, checking duct outside cowl every 6 months, not using chimney as storage shelf, and booking deep cleaning every 4 months.
